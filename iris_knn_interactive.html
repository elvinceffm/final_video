    Model intuition

    How KNN turns nearby flowers into a vote

    How KNN works

    KNN is a local vote among the nearest examples

    A new flower gets the label most common among the k closest training flowers.

    The manual schematic is better than a raw output table for teaching the idea. It shows that KNN is really a geometric vote, not a mysterious black box.

    The audience should understand the rule in one sentence: nearby points matter more than faraway points, and the majority class among the nearest neighbors wins.
